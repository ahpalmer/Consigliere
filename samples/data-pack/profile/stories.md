## Paging noise
- Situation: On-call engineers got ~25 pages a week, most not actionable.
- Task: Make pages trustworthy again.
- Action: Audited every alert, deleted or retuned the noisy ones, and wrote a runbook for each that remained.
- Result: Pages down 60%; time to acknowledge dropped by half.
- Themes: operational excellence, on-call, initiative.
